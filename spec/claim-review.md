# Claim Review Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing public OPE claims.

- Do not claim live calibration until the relevant domain, horizon, source policy, and coverage period have enough resolved comparable outcomes.
- Do not claim model lift without a baseline-lift report.
- Do not generalize fixture results to live performance.
- Do not claim support for unimplemented domains, geographies, APIs, protocols, payments, audits, or compliance outcomes.
- State sample size with every calibration or track-record claim.
- State whether a forecast is fixture-only, provisional live, resolved live, ambiguous, annulled, or excluded.
- Keep public examples free of secrets, private operational details, and raw prompt-like adversarial content.
